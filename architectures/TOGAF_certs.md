TOGAF® 10 Certification Study Plan
**Duration:** 4 Weeks  
**Target:** Level 1 (Foundation) + Level 2 (Certified) Combined Exam  

---

## Week 1: TOGAF Basics and Core Concepts

**Topics to Cover:**
- Introduction to Enterprise Architecture (EA)
- TOGAF Structure & Components
- Architecture Development Method (ADM) Overview
- TOGAF Definitions & Core Principles

**Resources:**
- TOGAF 10 Standard Overview: [Open Group Documentation](https://pubs.opengroup.org/architecture/togaf-standard)
- Official TOGAF Study Guide Chapters 1–3

**Activities:**
- Read and summarize key definitions
- Create flashcards for terms
- Take Level 1 practice quiz (basic concepts)

---

## Week 2: Detailed ADM Phases and Deliverables

**Topics to Cover:**
- ADM Phases: Preliminary, A, B, C, D
- ADM Phases: E, F, G, H, Requirements Management
- ADM Inputs, Steps, Outputs, and Deliverables

**Resources:**
- TOGAF Study Guide Chapters 4–8
- YouTube tutorials on TOGAF ADM walkthroughs
- ArchiMate basics (optional)

**Activities:**
- Create a diagram mapping the ADM cycle
- Note down deliverables for each ADM phase
- Take a practice test covering ADM phases

---

## Week 3: TOGAF Techniques, Governance, and Content Framework

**Topics to Cover:**
- Architecture Content Framework
- Architecture Views, Viewpoints, Stakeholders
- Building Blocks, Reference Models
- Architecture Governance
- Enterprise Continuum and Repository

**Resources:**
- TOGAF Study Guide Chapters 9–13
- ArchiMate modeling tool (optional: Archi or Visual Paradigm)

**Activities:**
- Model a simple Business, Application, and Technology architecture view
- Create sample deliverable templates
- Review case studies or sample scenarios

---

## Week 4: Practice Exams, Level 2 Scenarios, and Final Review

**Topics to Cover:**
- TOGAF Level 2 Scenario-based Questions
- Applying ADM in real-life scenarios
- ADM Techniques: Gap Analysis, Migration Planning
- Architecture Skills Framework (SFIA)

**Resources:**
- Official Open Group Practice Tests
- Udemy / Simplilearn mock exams
- TOGAF Level 2 sample scenario videos

**Activities:**
- Take full-length mock exams for both levels
- Review wrong answers and clarify concepts
- Focus on scenario-based reasoning

---

## Final Exam Readiness Checklist
- [ ] Completed all practice exams scoring >80%
- [ ] Reviewed all ADM phases and deliverables
- [ ] Understood governance and compliance processes
- [ ] Comfortable with scenario-based questions
- [ ] Reviewed ArchiMate (optional but recommended)
