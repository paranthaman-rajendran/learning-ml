
# 📅 TOGAF Part 1 Study Roadmap & Plan

## 📖 Week 1: Foundation & Core Concepts
- 📌 Introduction to Enterprise Architecture  
- 📌 Introduction to TOGAF Framework  
- 📌 The TOGAF Standard Structure ([TOGAF 10 Documentation](https://pubs.opengroup.org/togaf-standard))  
- 📌 TOGAF Core Concepts:
  - Architecture Views & Viewpoints  
  - Enterprise Continuum & Tools  
  - Architecture Repository  

🎯 **Deliverables:** Understand EA fundamentals, TOGAF structure, and key terminologies.

---

## 📖 Week 2: ADM & Architecture Content Framework
- 📌 Architecture Development Method (ADM)
  - Phases A–H
  - Requirements Management
  - ADM Guidelines and Techniques  
- 📌 Architecture Content Framework
  - Deliverables, Artifacts, Building Blocks  
- 📌 Enterprise Continuum & Architecture Repository (in-depth)

🎯 **Deliverables:** Know the ADM cycle, its objectives, inputs, outputs, and how content is organized.

---

## 📖 Week 3: Reference Models, Capability Framework, and Exam Prep
- 📌 TOGAF Reference Models:
  - TRM (Technical Reference Model)
  - III-RM (Integrated Information Infrastructure Reference Model)  
- 📌 TOGAF Capability Framework:
  - EA roles, skills, and responsibilities  
- 📌 Practice Mock Exams and Sample Questions  
- 📌 Review Weak Areas

🎯 **Deliverables:** Gain clarity on TOGAF resources, capability development, and boost confidence with mock exams.
